## Executive Summary

Based on 31:33 Jewelry's current stage—limited inventory (5-45 pieces per style), no proven sales track record, small team (Dana + 1-2 people), and tight budget—this 90-day roadmap prioritizes **proof of concept through concentrated, high-feedback channels** rather than attempting to launch all marketing channels simultaneously.

**Core Strategy**: Focus on 3 channels that provide immediate validation, generate cash flow, build essential systems, and create content/relationships that fuel future growth.

**Recommended Focus Channels for Days 1-90**:
1. **Christian Conferences & Events** (Primary - 50% of effort) → *See: Christian Conferences & Events Playbook*
2. **Customer Referral Program** (Secondary - 25% of effort) → *See: Affiliate & Referral Partnerships Playbook, Part A*
3. **Podcast Appearances** (Tertiary - 25% of effort) → *See: Podcast Appearances Playbook*

**Why These Three**: Events provide immediate sales validation and market research. Referral programs create systematic word-of-mouth with zero upfront cost. Podcasts build authority and awareness while Dana's on-air experience makes execution natural. All three work with current constraints and generate assets (customers, content, testimonials) that make other channels more effective later.

**What We're NOT Doing in First 90 Days**: Micro-influencer outreach, boutique retail partnerships, church partnerships, or affiliate networks (beyond the basic referral program). These channels are covered in the comprehensive playbooks and are better suited for Months 4-12 once you have proof of concept. You'll use the data and learnings from your first 90 days to determine which of these channels to activate next.



## Month 1: Foundation & First Sales (Days 1-30)

### Week 1-2: Setup & Preparation

**Primary Goal**: Get operational infrastructure in place for events and establish baseline systems.

#### Events Channel Setup (15 hours)
- **Identify 5-8 local/regional events** (church bazaars, women's ministry events, small Christian conferences within 2-hour drive)
  - Target: $25-400 booth fees
  - Timeline: Events happening in Months 1-3
  - Application: Submit to 3-4 events immediately
- **Create essential event materials**:
  - Simple 6ft table display setup (tablecloth, risers, signage)
  - Story cards (3x5"): "What does 31:33 mean?" with Jeremiah 31:33 explanation
  - Price tags/signage (professional but simple)
  - Square/payment processing setup
  - Email capture system (tablet + simple form or physical signup sheet)
- **Inventory allocation**: Reserve 40-50 pieces for first 2-3 events (mix of price points)
- **Event checklist**: Packing list, setup process, sales tracking sheet

**Owner**: Dana (strategy/design) + Team Member (execution/logistics)

#### Customer Referral Program Setup (8 hours)
- **Install ReferralCandy** (or similar) on Shopify
  - Configure: Friend gets 10% off, referrer gets $25 credit
  - Set up automated post-purchase emails
  - Create referral landing page
  - Design simple "share cards" to include in all shipments
- **Test thoroughly**: Place test orders, verify emails send, confirm tracking works
- **Launch quietly**: Turn on for all future orders (no big announcement yet)

**Owner**: Team Member (technical setup) + Dana (copy/messaging approval)

#### Podcast Guest Profile Creation (5 hours)
- **Create Dana's guest materials**:
  - 100/200/300-word bios
  - Professional headshot
  - 5-6 topic options with descriptions
  - Sample interview questions
  - Links to any previous media appearances
- **Set up PodMatch profile** (free tier to start)
- **Identify 10 target Tier 1 podcasts** (Christian motherhood/lifestyle shows, 5K-50K downloads)

**Owner**: Dana (content) + Team Member (platform setup/research)

### Week 3-4: First Event + First Podcast Pitch

#### Execute First Event (3-4 days including prep/travel/follow-up)
- **Attend 1-2 local events** (church bazaar or small women's ministry event)
- **Goals**: 
  - Sell 10-20 pieces
  - Test booth setup and sales process
  - Gather customer feedback (what questions do they ask? what objections?)
  - Collect 20-30 email addresses
  - Take photos/videos of customers (with permission)
  - Document what works and what doesn't
- **Post-event**:
  - Send thank-you emails to customers within 48 hours
  - Review sales data: which pieces sold? at what price points?
  - Adjust booth setup/messaging based on learnings

**Owner**: Dana (primary) + Team Member (logistics/support)

#### Launch Podcast Outreach (6 hours)
- **Send 5 personalized pitches** to Tier 1 target podcasts
- **Respond to 3-5 PodMatch opportunities** that fit brand
- **Goal**: Book 1-2 podcast appearances for Month 2

**Owner**: Team Member (research/drafting) + Dana (personalization/approval)

#### Referral Program Activation (2 hours)
- **Announce to existing email list** (if any): "Refer a friend, earn $25 credit"
- **Add to website prominently**: Homepage banner, account dashboard
- **Include in all event follow-up emails**: "Share 31:33 with friends who need to know they belong"

**Owner**: Team Member



## Month 2: Validation & Iteration (Days 31-60)

### Week 5-6: Scale Events + First Podcast Appearance

#### Attend 2-3 More Events (6-8 days total)

**Owner**: Team Member (creation) + Dana (approval/personal stories)

#### Referral Program Optimization (3 hours)
- **Review first 30 days of referral data**:
  - How many customers are referring?
  - What's the conversion rate of referred friends?
  - Are referral emails being opened?
- **Optimize based on data**:
  - Adjust email subject lines if open rates are low

- **Identify top referrers**: Send personal thank-you notes from Dana

**Owner**: Team Member

#### Podcast Pipeline Building (4 hours)

**Owner**: Team Member (outreach) + Dana (personalization)

## What We're NOT Doing in the First 90 Days (And Why)

This Marketing Action Plan includes comprehensive playbooks for six different channels. However, for your first 90 days, we're recommending you focus on just three. Here's why the other channels should wait—and when to activate them using the provided playbooks.

### Micro-Influencer Partnerships
**Why not now**: Requires significant outreach time, relationship building, and negotiation. Better to have proof of concept, customer testimonials, and professional content first. 

**When to add**: Months 4-6, once you have assets to share and can confidently say "We've sold 150+ pieces at events with 95% satisfaction."

**Where to learn more**: See the **Micro-Influencer Partnerships Playbook** for complete implementation guidance when you're ready.



### Boutique Retail Partnerships
**Why not now**: Requires line sheets, wholesale terms, inventory allocation, and ongoing relationship management. Better to prove retail viability at events first.

**When to add**: Months 4-6, approaching boutiques with "We've sold $40K+ at Christian women's events—here's the data."

**Where to learn more**: See the **Boutique Retail Partnerships Playbook** for complete implementation guidance when you're ready.



### Church Partnerships
**Why not now**: Longer sales cycles, requires customized proposals for each church, and competes for inventory with events.

**When to add**: Months 4-6, when event connections naturally surface church opportunities and you have bandwidth to nurture those relationships.

**Where to learn more**: See the **Church & Ministry Partnerships Playbook** for complete implementation guidance when you're ready.



### Affiliate Networks (ShareASale, etc.)
**Why not now**: $550 setup fee for ShareASale, requires comprehensive marketing materials, and takes 2-3 months to gain traction. The basic customer referral program (which you ARE implementing) provides the foundation.

**When to add**: Months 4-6, once referral program proves organic sharing works and you're ready to scale it to a formal affiliate network.

**Where to learn more**: See the **Affiliate & Referral Partnerships Playbook, Part B** for complete implementation guidance when you're ready.



### Paid Advertising
**Why not now**: Previous $7,000 campaign generated zero sales. You need proof of concept, refined messaging, and customer testimonials before spending on ads.

**When to add**: Months 7-12, once you have conversion data, testimonials, and retargeting audiences from other channels.

**Note**: Paid advertising is not covered in a separate playbook in this plan, as it's recommended for later-stage scaling after you've validated organic channels first.
