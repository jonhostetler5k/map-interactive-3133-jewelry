## Executive Summary

This Marketing Action Plan provides 31:33 Jewelry with a comprehensive, phased strategy to achieve proof of concept and build sustainable growth over the next 1-2 years. The plan is built on three strategic foundations:

**1. IMPACT Offering Analysis** - Validates that The Covenant Promise Collection is your primary offering with exceptional potential across all six IMPACT criteria (Interest, Margin, Potential, Attract, Competition, Tenure).

**2. IMPACT Prospect Definition** - Identifies your core customer as the "Covenant-Conscious Believer"—faith-forward individuals ages 28-65 seeking tangible expression of belonging through sophisticated, meaningful jewelry.

**3. 90-Day Roadmap** - Focuses your immediate efforts on three high-impact channels (Christian Conferences & Events, Customer Referral Program, Podcast Appearances) to generate proof of concept with minimal risk.

**4. Six Channel Playbooks** - Provides comprehensive implementation guides for scaling over 12-24 months across all major marketing channels.

### How to Use This Plan

**Start Here**: Read the 90-Day Roadmap and begin executing immediately on the three priority channels.

**Reference as Needed**: Use the relevant playbooks (Conferences & Events, Affiliate & Referral, Podcasts) for detailed implementation guidance during your first 90 days.

**Scale Strategically**: After 90 days, use your results to determine which additional channels to activate using the other playbooks (Micro-Influencer, Boutique Retail, Church Partnerships).

Think of this plan as your complete roadmap for the next 1-2 years of growth. You don't need to read everything at once—focus on what's immediately relevant, and return to the other sections as you're ready to expand.
