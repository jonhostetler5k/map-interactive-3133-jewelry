## Image Specifications

### Recommended Specs:
- **Format:** JPG or PNG (JPG preferred for photos)
- **Resolution:** 1920px width maximum (will scale responsively)
- **File Size:** Under 500KB per image (use compression if needed)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 4:5 (typical for social media ads)

### Image Optimization Tips:

If your images are very large, you can compress them using:
- **Online:** TinyPNG.com or Squoosh.app
- **Mac:** Preview app (Export → Reduce File Size)
- **Command Line:** `convert input.jpg -quality 85 output.jpg`
